Spotify Refine

Your Liked Songs,
finally sorted.

Refine learns what each of your playlists sounds like, files every stray liked song where it belongs, groups the rest into new playlists, then shows you your taste in ways Wrapped never does.

Log in with Spotify

One-time setup before you can log in

  1. Create an app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard.
  2. Add the redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback to it.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env, paste the Client ID and secret, and restart python -m src.app.

Development-mode Spotify apps work for up to 5 accounts you add on the dashboard. Nothing leaves your machine except calls to Spotify and Last.fm.

  • Sort every unsorted liked song into the playlist it fits, with a confidence for each.
  • Discover new playlists hiding in the songs that fit nowhere.
